# The Getovr Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity

Brand Name

The Getovr

Tagline

Wear Your Identity.

---

# Mission

To empower people to express their individuality through premium customized apparel.

---

# Vision

Build India's most trusted premium custom clothing brand.

---

# Core Values

- Creativity
- Quality
- Simplicity
- Transparency
- Customer First
- Innovation

---

# Brand Personality

The Getovr should feel:

- Modern
- Premium
- Minimal
- Confident
- Bold
- Creative

---

# Tone of Voice

Always write in a tone that is:

- Friendly
- Clear
- Professional
- Motivational

Avoid:

- Overly corporate language
- Slang-heavy copy
- Clickbait
- Excessive emojis

---

# Logo Usage

Logo should:

- Maintain clear spacing
- Never be stretched
- Never be rotated
- Never be recolored outside approved palette

---

# Color Palette

Primary

Black

White

Secondary

Charcoal Gray

Accent

Use accent colors sparingly for calls-to-action and highlights.

---

# Typography

Primary

Modern sans-serif

Characteristics

- Clean
- Readable
- Premium
- Consistent

---

# Photography Style

Use imagery that is:

- High resolution
- Authentic
- Product-focused
- Lifestyle-oriented
- Minimal background clutter

---

# Product Mockups

Mockups should:

- Display products clearly
- Maintain realistic proportions
- Use consistent lighting
- Avoid excessive effects

---

# Packaging Guidelines

Packaging should communicate:

- Premium quality
- Minimalism
- Sustainability where possible

Include:

- Brand logo
- Thank-you card
- Care instructions

---

# Social Media Guidelines

Content Pillars

- Product showcases
- Behind-the-scenes
- Customer stories
- Design inspiration
- Brand updates

Visual Style

- Consistent colors
- Clean layouts
- Strong typography
- High-quality imagery

---

# Customer Communication

All customer communication should be:

- Respectful
- Prompt
- Helpful
- Honest

---

# Marketing Principles

Focus on:

- Quality over quantity
- Community building
- Authentic storytelling
- Customer trust

Avoid:

- Misleading discounts
- False urgency
- Unrealistic claims

---

# Brand Consistency Checklist

Before publishing any content, verify:

- Brand colors are correct
- Typography is consistent
- Logo usage follows guidelines
- Messaging aligns with brand voice
- Product imagery meets quality standards

---

# Future Brand Evolution

As The Getovr grows, this guide will expand to include:

- Campaign guidelines
- Influencer collaborations
- Video production standards
- Retail branding
- Event branding
- International localization
