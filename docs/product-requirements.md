# The Getovr Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Overview

The Getovr is a premium custom apparel platform that enables customers to design, personalize, and purchase high-quality clothing through an intuitive online design studio.

The platform combines e-commerce, product customization, secure payments, and production management into one seamless experience.

---

# Vision

To become India's most trusted platform for premium custom apparel and personalized fashion.

---

# Mission

Empower everyone to express themselves through high-quality customized clothing while providing an effortless buying experience.

Customers struggle to order customized apparel because existing platforms are:

# Functional Requirements

The system shall:

- Support custom product design.
- Allow secure payments.
- Store order information.
- Provide order tracking.
- Support admin management.
- Manage production workflow.

# Out of Scope (MVP)

The following are intentionally excluded from the first release:

- Marketplace
- Vendor onboarding
- Multi-language support
- Subscription plans
- International shipping
- Native mobile application

# Long-Term Vision

The Getovr should evolve into a complete custom merchandise ecosystem supporting creators, businesses, and organizations with scalable production, logistics, analytics, and AI-powered design tools.
